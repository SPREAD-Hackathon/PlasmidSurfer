Plasmid relatedness explorer

No plasmid selected

Click a plasmid in the graph, or type its name in Find. Links join plasmids PLING considers related; shorter DCJ distance means fewer structural rearrangements apart.

BLAST a plasmid sequence

Runs blastn (megablast) against all plasmid sequences in the database and lists the best hits. Click a hit to find it in the graph, or show all hits at once.

The BLAST search needs the small BLAST server, and this page was opened without it. For the Klebsiella view, double-click start_klebsiella.command in the explorer folder; it starts the server and opens the page at http://localhost:8000. Otherwise run python3 server.py --web <folder> --db <database> (see the README) and open http://localhost:8000. Opening index.html directly from disk shows the graph only.
